# Accessibility / 可访问性

## Reading and controls

Glim offers selectable text, source search and adjustable reading layouts where supported. Ebooks have typography controls; document and image controls vary by reader. Settings → Keyboard Shortcuts lists supported commands. See the [user guide](docs/user-guide.md) for navigation and reading actions.

Current builds target Apple Silicon and Intel Macs. Full VoiceOver, keyboard-only, high-contrast and reduced-motion coverage has not been validated, and conformance to an accessibility standard is not claimed. Native previews depend partly on system controls. A shortcut or system control being present does not prove the entire workflow is accessible.

[Report a barrier](https://github.com/glimapp/glim/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) with the app/macOS version, assistive technology, task, expected behavior and where you became blocked. Sanitized examples are welcome; medical or personal information is unnecessary.

## 中文说明

Glim 在支持的阅读器中提供文字选择、源码查找和阅读布局调整；电子书可调整字号等排版。各文档与图片的操作不同，设置中的“键盘快捷键”提供命令说明，具体操作见[使用指南](docs/user-guide.zh-CN.md)。

当前尚未完整验证 VoiceOver、纯键盘导航、高对比度与减少动态效果，也不声明符合某项可访问性标准。原生预览部分依赖系统控件，存在快捷键不代表整个流程已可无障碍完成。

反馈时请描述使用的辅助技术、版本、正在完成的任务和具体受阻位置；无需提供医疗或个人资料。
