# Code of Conduct

Glim welcomes people who use the app, report problems, write documentation and contribute code, across backgrounds and experience levels. English and Chinese are welcome throughout the project.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful and constructive. Discuss the work, provide useful context, credit others, and accept reasonable feedback. Respect people's privacy and time.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexual harassment, doxxing, spam, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Do not publish another person's private information without permission.

## Scope and enforcement

These expectations apply to this repository's issues, pull requests, discussions, and other official Glim community spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, warn participants, or restrict participation. Responses should reflect the seriousness and repetition of the behavior; maintainers are also subject to these expectations.

To report harmful content, use GitHub's **Report content** control on the relevant item. For concerns that can safely be discussed publicly, contact the maintainer [@NeoPanic](https://github.com/NeoPanic) in the relevant thread. Do not post sensitive evidence in a public issue. Private conduct reporting currently relies on GitHub's reporting channels; the project does not advertise a separate private mailbox.

## 中文说明

欢迎使用软件、报告问题、编写文档和贡献代码的参与者，中文与英文均可。请就事论事，补充有用背景、尊重他人的贡献、隐私和时间。

禁止骚扰、威胁、歧视、性骚扰、泄露隐私、垃圾信息和恶意干扰。未经允许不要公开他人的私密信息。

这些约定适用于本仓库的 Issue、PR、Discussions 及其他官方社区空间，维护者同样遵守。维护者可按行为的严重程度及重复情况移除内容、警告或限制参与。

敏感举报使用相关内容上的 GitHub **Report content**。适合公开讨论的问题可以在对应话题中联系维护者 [@NeoPanic](https://github.com/NeoPanic)。不要公开私密证据；当前没有单独公布的私密举报邮箱。
